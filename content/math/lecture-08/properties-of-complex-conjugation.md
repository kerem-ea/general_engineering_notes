# Properties of Complex Conjugation

## Algebraic Properties

For all $z, z_1, z_2 \in \mathbb{C}$ and $n \in \mathbb{Z}$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\overline{\overline{z}} &= z \\
\overline{z_1 + z_2} &= \overline{z_1} + \overline{z_2} \\
\overline{z_1 \cdot z_2} &= \overline{z_1} \cdot \overline{z_2} \\
\overline{\left(\frac{1}{z}\right)} &= \frac{1}{\overline{z}} \quad (z \neq 0) \\
\overline{z^n} &= (\overline{z})^n
\end{aligned}
$$

## Subset Characterization

$$
z \in \mathbb{R} \iff \overline{z} = z
$$

$$
z \in i\mathbb{R} \iff \overline{z} = -z
$$

## Conjugation and Polar Form

For $z = |z|e^{i\arg(z)}$ and $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$:

$$
\overline{e^z} = e^{\overline{z}}
$$

$$
\overline{e^{i\alpha}} = e^{-i\alpha}
$$

$$
\overline{z} = |z|e^{-i\arg(z)} \implies |\overline{z}| = |z|, \quad \arg(\overline{z}) \equiv -\arg(z) \pmod{2\pi}
$$
