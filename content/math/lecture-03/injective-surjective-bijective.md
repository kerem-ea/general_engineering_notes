# Injective, Surjective and Bijective

## Injective

A function is **injective** if different inputs always give different outputs:

$$
a_1\neq a_2\implies f(a_1)\neq f(a_2)
$$

Equivalently:

$$
f(a_1)=f(a_2)\implies a_1=a_2
$$

## Surjective

A function is **surjective** if every element of the codomain is reached:

$$
\forall b\in B,\ \exists a\in A:f(a)=b
$$

Equivalently:

$$
f(A)=B
$$

## Bijective

A function is **bijective** if it is both injective and surjective:

$$
f\text{ bijective}
\iff
f\text{ injective}\land f\text{ surjective}
$$
