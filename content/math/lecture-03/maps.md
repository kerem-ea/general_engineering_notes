# Maps

(book section 2.2, pages 22-24)

A **map** (or **function**) $f$ assigns exactly one output to every input:

$$
f: A \to B
$$

$$
x \mapsto f(x)
$$

> [!note] Terminology
> The terms *map* and *[[functions|function]]* are synonyms in standard mathematics and are used interchangeably.
