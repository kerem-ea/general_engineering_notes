## Overview

This lecture introduces spectral theory for linear maps and square matrices:
- Formulating the eigenvalue problem $Av = \lambda v$ and extracting eigenvalues via roots of the characteristic polynomial $p_A(Z) = \det(A - Z I_n)$.
- Establishing eigenspaces $E_\lambda = \ker(A - \lambda I_n)$ as invariant subspaces and computing bases for them via Gaussian elimination.
- Distinguishing algebraic multiplicity $\mathrm{am}(\lambda)$ from geometric multiplicity $\mathrm{gm}(\lambda)$ and proving the fundamental inequality $1 \le \mathrm{gm}(\lambda) \le \mathrm{am}(\lambda) \le n$.
