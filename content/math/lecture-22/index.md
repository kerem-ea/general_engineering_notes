## Overview

This lecture covers the theory and mechanics of matrix and operator diagonalization:
- Constructing an eigenbasis and formulating the matrix diagonalization identity $P^{-1} A P = D$.
- Applying diagonalization to compute matrix powers $A^k = P D^k P^{-1}$ and analytic matrix functions.
- Establishing necessary and sufficient criteria for diagonalizability through polynomial splitting and geometric multiplicity matching: $\mathrm{gm}(\lambda) = \mathrm{am}(\lambda)$.
- Formalizing similarity of matrices ($B = P^{-1} A P$) and identifying structural similarity invariants including trace, determinant, characteristic polynomial, and spectrum.
