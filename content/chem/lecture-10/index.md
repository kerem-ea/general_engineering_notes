## Overview

This lecture bridges macroscopic thermodynamics and electrochemical devices:
- Characterizing entropy, microstate distributions, and the Second Law criterion $\Delta S_{\mathrm{univ}} \ge 0$.
- Deriving Gibbs free energy $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$ and its direct relationship to chemical equilibrium: $\Delta G^\circ = -RT \ln K$.
- Analyzing galvanic cell architectures, electrode polarity, and line notation shorthand.
- Calculating standard cell potentials from standard reduction tables and utilizing the Nernst equation for non-standard electrochemical potentials.
- Investigating commercial battery chemistries, the electrochemical mechanisms of iron corrosion, and quantitative electrolytic reactions using Faraday's laws.
