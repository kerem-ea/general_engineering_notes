# Reaction Conditions

Special reaction conditions (heat, light, catalysts, temperature, pressure) are written **above or below the reaction arrow** ($\rightarrow$).

## Common Notations

- **Heat ($\Delta$):** Thermal energy required for reaction.
  $$CaCO_3(s) \xrightarrow{\Delta} CaO(s) + CO_2(g)$$
- **Light ($h\nu$):** Photochemical initiation.
- **Catalyst:** Species that increases reaction rate without being consumed.
  $$2H_2O_2(aq) \xrightarrow{\text{catalyst}} 2H_2O(l) + O_2(g)$$
- **Specific Conditions:** Temperature, pressure, or solvent specified directly over arrow (e.g., $\xrightarrow{200^\circ\text{C},\ 50\text{ atm}}$).
