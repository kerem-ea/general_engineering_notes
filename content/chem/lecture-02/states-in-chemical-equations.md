# States in Chemical Equations

Physical states of reactants and products are designated in parentheses directly following their chemical formulas:

| Symbol | State | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `(s)` | Solid | Solid substance |
| `(l)` | Liquid | Pure liquid |
| `(g)` | Gas | Gaseous substance |
| `(aq)` | Aqueous | Dissolved in water (aqueous solution) |

## Example

$$
CH_4(g) + 2O_2(g) \rightarrow CO_2(g) + 2H_2O(l)
$$

$$
NaCl(s) \xrightarrow{H_2O} Na^+(aq) + Cl^-(aq)
$$

> State symbols indicate physical state; they do not alter chemical stoichiometry or atom balance.
