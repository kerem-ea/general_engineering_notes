# Chemical Equations

A **chemical equation** represents a chemical reaction by showing the identities and relative quantities of reactants and products:

$$
\text{Reactants} \rightarrow \text{Products}
$$

- **Reactants**: Initial substances consumed (left of arrow).
- **Products**: Substances formed (right of arrow).
- **Coefficients**: Numbers placed before formulas indicating relative molar amounts (a coefficient of $1$ is omitted).
- **Plus sign (`+`)**: Separates distinct reactant or product species.
- **Arrow ($\rightarrow$)**: Indicates direction of chemical transformation.

$$
CH_4 + 2O_2 \rightarrow CO_2 + 2H_2O
$$

> Equations must obey the **law of conservation of matter**—the number of atoms of each element must remain constant across the reaction.
