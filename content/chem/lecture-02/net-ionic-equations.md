# Net Ionic Equations

A **net ionic equation** includes only the chemical species (ions and molecules) directly participating in the chemical change, eliminating all spectator ions.

## Derivation Steps

1. **Balanced Molecular Equation:**
   $$CaCl_2(aq) + 2AgNO_3(aq) \rightarrow Ca(NO_3)_2(aq) + 2AgCl(s)$$
2. **Complete Ionic Equation** (dissociate soluble strong electrolytes):
   $$Ca^{2+}(aq) + 2Cl^-(aq) + 2Ag^+(aq) + 2NO_3^-(aq) \rightarrow Ca^{2+}(aq) + 2NO_3^-(aq) + 2AgCl(s)$$
3. **Cancel Spectator Ions** ($Ca^{2+}, NO_3^-$) and simplify:
   $$Ag^+(aq) + Cl^-(aq) \rightarrow AgCl(s)$$

## Summary of Equation Formats

| Format | Representation |
|---|---|
| **Molecular** | Neutral compound formulas |
| **Complete Ionic** | All dissociated aqueous ions + intact solids/liquids/gases |
| **Net Ionic** | Only species undergoing reaction |

> Net ionic equations must balance in **both atom count and total charge**.
