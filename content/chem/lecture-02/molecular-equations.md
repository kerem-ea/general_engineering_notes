# Molecular Equations

A **molecular equation** represents a reaction using the complete neutral chemical formulas of all reactants and products:

$$
CaCl_2(aq) + 2AgNO_3(aq) \rightarrow Ca(NO_3)_2(aq) + 2AgCl(s)
$$

- **Utility:** Standard format for stoichiometric and mass-based calculations.
- **Limitation:** Does not reflect that soluble ionic compounds exist as dissociated free ions in solution (addressed by **ionic equations**).
