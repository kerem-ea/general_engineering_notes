# Reaction Yields

## Limiting and Excess Reactants

- **Limiting Reactant:** The reactant completely consumed first; sets the theoretical upper limit on product yield.
- **Excess Reactant:** Reactant remaining after the limiting reactant is entirely consumed.
- **Determination:** Calculate moles of product possible from each available reactant; the reactant yielding the least product is limiting.

## Yields

- **Theoretical Yield:** Maximum product predicted by stoichiometry assuming 100% completion.
- **Actual Yield:** Amount of product experimentally collected ($\text{Actual} \le \text{Theoretical}$ due to incomplete reactions, competing side reactions, or isolation losses).
- **Percent Yield:**
  $$
  \text{Percent Yield} = \frac{\text{Actual Yield}}{\text{Theoretical Yield}} \times 100\%
  $$

## Atom Economy

Evaluates the theoretical efficiency of a synthetic reaction route (green chemistry):

$$
\text{Atom Economy} = \frac{\text{Molar mass of desired product}}{\sum \text{Molar masses of all reactants}} \times 100\%
$$

- *Distinction:* **Atom economy** assesses the inherent route design; **percent yield** assesses experimental execution.
