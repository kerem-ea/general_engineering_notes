# States of Matter and Density

## States of matter

The common states of matter are governed by the balance between thermal kinetic energy and [[intermolecular-forces|intermolecular forces]]:

- **Solid:** particles are closely arranged in structured lattices; the substance has a defined shape and volume.
- **Liquid:** particles can move relative to one another; the substance has a defined volume but takes the shape of its container.
- **Gas:** particles are widely separated and expand to fill their container (see [[chem/lecture-02/gas-pressure|Gas Pressure]] and [[chem/lecture-02/ideal-gas-law-expanded|Ideal Gas Law]]).

In thermodynamics, phase changes directly affect system entropy (see [[future_energy/lecture-02/second-law-and-entropy#Entropy by Phase|Entropy by Phase]]).

## Density

Density is mass per unit volume:

$$
\rho = \frac{m}{V}
$$

where:

- $\rho$ is density
- $m$ is mass (see [[moles-and-mass|Moles and Mass]])
- $V$ is volume

Rearranging:

$$
m = \rho V
$$

and

$$
V = \frac{m}{\rho}
$$
