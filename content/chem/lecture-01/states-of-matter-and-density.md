# States of Matter and Density

## States of matter

The common states of matter are:

- **Solid** — particles are closely arranged and the substance has a defined shape and volume.
- **Liquid** — particles can move relative to one another; the substance has a defined volume but takes the shape of its container.
- **Gas** — particles are widely separated and the substance expands to fill its container.

## Density

Density is mass per unit volume:

$$
\rho = \frac{m}{V}
$$

where:

- \(\rho\) is density
- \(m\) is mass
- \(V\) is volume

Rearranging:

$$
m = \rho V
$$

and

$$
V = \frac{m}{\rho}
$$
