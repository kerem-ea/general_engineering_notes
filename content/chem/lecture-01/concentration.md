# Concentration

Molar concentration describes the amount of dissolved substance per unit volume of solution.

$$
c = \frac{n}{V}
$$

Using

$$
n = \frac{m}{M}
$$

gives

$$
c = \frac{m}{MV}
$$

Rearranging:

$$
m = cMV
$$

where:

- $c$ = concentration
- $n$ = amount of substance
- $V$ = volume
- $m$ = mass
- $M$ = molar mass

## Dilution

When solvent is added to dilute a solution, the amount of solute remains constant:

$$
n_1 = n_2
$$

Because $n = cV$:

$$
c_1 V_1 = c_2 V_2
$$

where:

- $c_1, V_1$ = initial concentration and volume
- $c_2, V_2$ = final concentration and volume
