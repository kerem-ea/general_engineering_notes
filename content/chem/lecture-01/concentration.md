# Concentration

Molar concentration describes the amount of dissolved substance per unit volume of solution.

$$
c = \frac{n}{V}
$$

Using

$$
n = \frac{m}{M}
$$

gives

$$
c = \frac{m}{MV}
$$

Rearranging:

$$
m = cMV
$$

where:

- \(c\) = concentration
- \(n\) = amount of substance
- \(V\) = volume
- \(m\) = mass
- \(M\) = molar mass
