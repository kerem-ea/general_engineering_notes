# Extensive and Intensive Properties

Properties of matter can be either extensive or intensive.

## Extensive properties

Depend on the amount of substance.

Examples:

- Mass
- Volume
- Number of particles
- Moles

$$m=100 g→200 g$$
More substance = a greater value.

## Intensive properties

Do not depend on the amount of substance.

Examples:

- Density
- Temperature
- Pressure
- Melting point
- Boiling point

The easiest way to remember:

**Extensive = depends on amount**  
**Intensive = independent of amount**
