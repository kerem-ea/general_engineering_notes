# Unit Conversions

## Distance

| Imperial | Metric |
|---|---:|
| 1 inch (in) | 2.54 cm |
| 1 foot (ft) | 30.48 cm |
| 1 yard (yd) | 0.9144 m |
| 1 mile (mi) | 1.609 km |

## Volume (US)

| Imperial | Metric |
|---|---:|
| 1 fl oz | 29.57 mL |
| 1 cup | 236.59 mL |
| 1 pint (pt) | 473.18 mL |
| 1 quart (qt) | 0.946 L |
| 1 gallon (gal) | 3.785 L |

|---|---:|

## Speed

| Imperial | Metric |
|---|---:|
| 1 mph | 1.609 km/h |

## Conversion Formulas

$$
\text{Metric} = \text{Imperial} \times \text{Factor}
$$

$$
\text{Imperial} = \frac{\text{Metric}}{\text{Factor}}
$$

## Important Distinctions

- oz = mass
- fl oz = volume
- ft = length
- ft² = area
- ft³ = volume
- US and UK gallons are different.
- US and UK fluid ounces are different.
