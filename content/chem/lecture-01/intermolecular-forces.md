# Intermolecular Forces

Intermolecular forces are attractions between molecules. They influence properties such as boiling point, melting point, viscosity, and solubility.

## London dispersion forces

London dispersion forces arise from temporary fluctuations in electron distribution. They occur between all atoms and molecules.

## Dipole-dipole interactions

Dipole-dipole interactions occur between molecules with permanent electric dipoles.

## Hydrogen bonding

Hydrogen bonding is a particularly strong type of intermolecular interaction involving hydrogen bonded to highly electronegative atoms, commonly nitrogen, oxygen, or fluorine, and an electronegative atom on a neighboring molecule.
