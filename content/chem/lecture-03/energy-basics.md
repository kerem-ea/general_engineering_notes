# Energy Basics

## Energy and Work

- **Energy ($E$):** Capacity to do work or supply heat.
- **Kinetic Energy:** Energy of particle motion (see [[future_energy/lecture-01/kinetic-energy|Kinetic Energy]]):
  $$
  E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2
  $$
- **Potential Energy:** Stored chemical energy in [[chem/lecture-01/chemical-bonds|chemical bonds]] and [[chem/lecture-01/intermolecular-forces|intermolecular forces]].

## Heat vs Temperature

- **Temperature ($T$):** Intensive measure of average particle kinetic energy ($\text{K}$ or $^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Heat ($q$):** Thermal energy transferred due to $\Delta T$ (hot $\to$ cold).

## Sign Convention for Heat ($q$)

- **Exothermic ($q < 0$):** System releases heat to surroundings ($T_{\text{surr}}$ rises).
- **Endothermic ($q > 0$):** System absorbs heat from surroundings ($T_{\text{surr}}$ falls).

## Energy Units and Conversions

$$
1\text{ cal} = 4.184\text{ J}
$$
$$
1\text{ Cal (food)} = 1\text{ kcal} = 1000\text{ cal} = 4.184\text{ kJ}
$$
$$
1\text{ J} = 1\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{s}^2 = 1\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}
$$
