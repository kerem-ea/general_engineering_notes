# Standard Enthalpy of Combustion

## Definition and Equation

**Standard enthalpy of combustion ($\Delta H^\circ_c$)** is the heat released when **$1\text{ mole}$** of fuel burns completely in $O_2$ at standard conditions ($1\text{ bar}$, $298.15\text{ K}$):

$$
\text{Fuel} + O_2(g) \to \text{Combustion Products} \quad (\Delta H^\circ_c < 0)
$$

Written for exactly $1\text{ mole}$ of fuel:
- $CH_4(g) + 2O_2(g) \to CO_2(g) + 2H_2O(l) \quad \Delta H^\circ_c = -890.8\text{ kJ/mol}$
- $C_8H_{18}(l) + \frac{25}{2}O_2(g) \to 8CO_2(g) + 9H_2O(l) \quad \Delta H^\circ_c = -5465.5\text{ kJ/mol}$

## Stoichiometric Energy Calculation

$$
q = n_{\text{fuel}} \times \Delta H^\circ_c = \frac{m}{M} \times \Delta H^\circ_c = \frac{\rho V}{M} \times \Delta H^\circ_c
$$

- $m$ = fuel mass ($\text{g}$)
- $M$ = fuel molar mass ($\text{g/mol}$)
- $\rho$ = density ($\text{g/mL}$)
- $V$ = volume ($\text{mL}$)

## Key Values ($298.15\text{ K}$)

| Fuel | Formula | $\Delta H^\circ_c$ ($\text{kJ/mol}$) |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon (graphite) | $C(s)$ | $-393.5$ |
| Hydrogen | $H_2(g)$ | $-285.8$ |
| Methane | $CH_4(g)$ | $-890.8$ |
| Acetylene | $C_2H_2(g)$ | $-1301.1$ |
| Ethanol | $C_2H_5OH(l)$ | $-1366.8$ |
| Isooctane | $C_8H_{18}(l)$ | $-5465.5$ |
