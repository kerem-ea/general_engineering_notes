# Heat Capacity and Specific Heat

## Core Equations

- **Heat Capacity ($C$, extensive):**
  $$
  C = \frac{q}{\Delta T} \quad [\text{J}/^\circ\text{C} \text{ or } \text{J}/\text{K}]
  $$
- **Specific Heat Capacity ($c$, intensive):**
  $$
  c = \frac{q}{m\Delta T} \quad [\text{J}/(\text{g}\cdot^\circ\text{C})]
  $$
- **Heat Transfer Equation:**
  $$
  q = c m \Delta T = c m (T_f - T_i)
  $$
  - $q$ = heat ($\text{J}$)
  - $c$ = specific heat ($\text{J}/(\text{g}\cdot^\circ\text{C})$)
  - $m$ = mass ($\text{g}$)
  - $\Delta T = T_f - T_i$ = temperature change ($^\circ\text{C}$ or $\text{K}$)

## Mathematical Signs of $q$

- $\Delta T > 0 \implies T_f > T_i \implies q > 0$ (heat gained / endothermic)
- $\Delta T < 0 \implies T_f < T_i \implies q < 0$ (heat lost / exothermic)

## Algebraic Rearrangements

$$
c = \frac{q}{m\Delta T} \qquad m = \frac{q}{c\Delta T} \qquad \Delta T = \frac{q}{c m}
$$

## Key Reference Values

- $c_{\text{water}(l)} = 4.184\text{ J}/(\text{g}\cdot^\circ\text{C})$
- $c_{\text{ice}} = 2.093\text{ J}/(\text{g}\cdot^\circ\text{C})$
- $c_{\text{Al}} = 0.897\text{ J}/(\text{g}\cdot^\circ\text{C})$
- $c_{\text{Fe}} = 0.449\text{ J}/(\text{g}\cdot^\circ\text{C})$
- $c_{\text{Cu}} = 0.385\text{ J}/(\text{g}\cdot^\circ\text{C})$
