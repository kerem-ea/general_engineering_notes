# Quantum Numbers

Four quantum numbers specify the state, energy, shape, orientation, and spin of an electron in an atom.

## The Four Quantum Numbers

| Name | Symbol | Allowed Values | Physical Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Principal** | $n$ | $1, 2, 3, \dots$ | Shell number, orbital size, and primary energy level. |
| **Angular Momentum (Azimuthal)** | $l$ | $0, 1, \dots, n - 1$ | Subshell type and geometric shape of the orbital. |
| **Magnetic** | $m_l$ | $-l, \dots, 0, \dots, +l$ | Spatial orientation of the orbital ($2l + 1$ orbitals per subshell). |
| **Spin** | $m_s$ | $+\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{1}{2}$ | Direction of intrinsic electron spin (Samuel Goudsmit and George Uhlenbeck). |

## Subshell Designations and Shapes

- **$l = 0$ ($s$ orbital):** Spherical. 1 orbital per shell ($m_l = 0$).
- **$l = 1$ ($p$ orbital):** Dumbbell-shaped with two lobes. 3 orbitals per shell ($p_x, p_y, p_z$).
- **$l = 2$ ($d$ orbital):** Four-lobed or dumbbell with torus. 5 orbitals per shell.
- **$l = 3$ ($f$ orbital):** Multi-lobed. 7 orbitals per shell.

For shell $n$:
- Total orbitals $= n^2$
- Maximum electrons $= 2n^2$

## Orbital Degeneracy

- **Degenerate Orbitals:** Orbitals possessing the exact same energy level.
- **One-electron systems (Hydrogen):** Orbital energy depends only on $n$. All subshells within shell $n$ are degenerate.
- **Multi-electron atoms:** Electron-electron repulsions remove subshell degeneracy within the same shell:
  $$
  E_s < E_p < E_d < E_f
  $$
  Orbitals within the same subshell remain degenerate unless an external magnetic field is applied.
