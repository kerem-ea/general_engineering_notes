# Ideal Gas Law

The ideal gas equation of state describes the thermodynamic state of a hypothetical ideal gas:

$$
pV = nRT
$$

where:

- $p$ = absolute pressure ($\mathrm{Pa}$)
- $V$ = volume ($\mathrm{m^3}$)
- $n$ = amount of substance ($\mathrm{mol}$)
- $R \approx 8.314\ \mathrm{J/(mol \cdot K)}$ = universal gas constant
- $T$ = absolute temperature ($\mathrm{K}$)

## Assumptions of the ideal gas model

1. Gas molecules have negligible volume compared to the total volume of the container.
2. Intermolecular forces between particles are negligible except during collisions.
3. All collisions between particles and with container walls are completely elastic.
