# Newton's Laws of Motion

Newton's laws form the classical mechanical foundation for analysing forces, motion, and mechanical energy conversion.

## First law (Law of Inertia)

An object remains at rest or moves at a constant velocity in a straight line unless acted upon by a net external force:

$$
\sum \mathbf{F} = 0 \implies \mathbf{v} = \text{constant}
$$

## Second law (Fundamental Law of Dynamics)

The net force acting on a body equals the time rate of change of its momentum, or mass multiplied by acceleration for constant mass:

$$
\mathbf{F}_{\mathrm{net}} = m\mathbf{a}
$$

## Third law (Action and Reaction)

When one body exerts a force on a second body, the second body exerts an equal and opposite force on the first body:

$$
\mathbf{F}_{A \to B} = -\mathbf{F}_{B \to A}
$$
