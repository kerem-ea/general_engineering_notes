# Gravitational Energy

Gravitational energy is the potential energy associated with gravitational attraction between masses.

## Universal gravitation

Newton's law of universal gravitation gives the gravitational attractive force between two point masses $m_1$ and $m_2$ separated by distance $r$:

$$
F = G\frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}
$$

where $G \approx 6.674 \times 10^{-11}\ \mathrm{N\cdot m^2/kg^2}$ is the gravitational constant.

## Gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface

For an object of mass $m$ at height $h$ relative to an arbitrary reference datum in a uniform gravitational field:

$$
E_{\mathrm{pot}} = mgh
$$

where $g \approx 9.81\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$ is the local gravitational acceleration.
