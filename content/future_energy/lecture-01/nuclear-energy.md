# Nuclear Energy

Nuclear energy is released or absorbed during changes in the atomic nucleus, including nuclear fission, nuclear fusion, and radioactive decay.

## Mass-energy equivalence

Mass and energy are fundamentally equivalent according to Einstein's mass-energy equation:

$$
E = mc^2
$$

where $c \approx 3.00 \times 10^8\ \mathrm{m/s}$ is the speed of light in vacuum.

Because $c^2 \approx 9 \times 10^{16}\ \mathrm{m^2/s^2}$, a minuscule difference in nuclear rest mass (mass defect $\Delta m$) corresponds to an immense release of energy:

$$
\Delta E = \Delta m \, c^2
$$
