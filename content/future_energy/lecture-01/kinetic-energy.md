# Kinetic Energy

Kinetic energy is the energy possessed by an object due to its motion.

## Translational kinetic energy

For a body of mass $m$ moving with linear velocity $v$:

$$
E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$

![[kinetic_energy.png]]

## Rotational kinetic energy

For a body rotating about a fixed axis with moment of inertia $I$ and angular velocity $\omega$ (in $\mathrm{rad/s}$):

$$
E_{k,\mathrm{rot}} = \frac{1}{2}I\omega^2
$$
