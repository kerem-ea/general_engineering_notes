# Heat and Expansion Work

Thermodynamics distinguishes between ordered and disordered forms of energy transfer.

## Ordered vs disordered energy

- **Heat ($Q$):** Energy transferred across a boundary purely due to a temperature gradient. Heat is disordered microscopic kinetic motion.
- **Work ($W$):** Energy transferred via an organized macroscopic mechanism, such as a piston movement against pressure or an electric current.

## Pressure and boundary work

Pressure ($p$) is normal force applied per unit area:

$$
p = \frac{F}{A}
$$

The SI unit of pressure is the pascal ($1\ \mathrm{Pa} = 1\ \mathrm{N/m^2}$).

For a closed thermodynamic system expanding against external pressure $p_{\mathrm{ext}}$, the differential boundary work is:

$$
\delta W = p_{\mathrm{ext}}\,dV
$$

Total work over a finite volume change is:

$$
W = \int_{V_1}^{V_2} p_{\mathrm{ext}}\,dV
$$
