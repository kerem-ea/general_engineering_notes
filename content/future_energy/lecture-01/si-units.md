# SI Units

A consistent system of physical units is essential for thermodynamic and energy calculations.

![[si_units.png]]

Key energy-related base and derived SI units:

- **Energy / Work:** Joule ($\mathrm{J} = \mathrm{N \cdot m} = \mathrm{kg \cdot m^2 / s^2}$)
- **Power:** Watt ($\mathrm{W} = \mathrm{J / s} = \mathrm{kg \cdot m^2 / s^3}$)
- **Force:** Newton ($\mathrm{N} = \mathrm{kg \cdot m / s^2}$)
- **Pressure:** Pascal ($\mathrm{Pa} = \mathrm{N / m^2} = \mathrm{kg / (m \cdot s^2)}$)
- **Temperature:** Kelvin ($\mathrm{K}$)
