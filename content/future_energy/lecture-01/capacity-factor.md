# Capacity Factor

The **capacity factor (CF)** is a dimensionless measure of the actual energy output of a power generation facility over a given period divided by the maximum possible output if operated continuously at rated nominal power.

## Mathematical definition

$$
\mathrm{CF} = \frac{\text{actual energy produced}}{\text{rated power} \times \text{time}}
$$

For a time interval $[0, T]$ with time-varying power production $P_{\mathrm{actual}}(t)$:

$$
\mathrm{CF} = \frac{\int_0^T P_{\mathrm{actual}}(t)\,dt}{P_{\mathrm{rated}} \cdot T}
$$

Capacity factor varies by technology (e.g. baseload nuclear power plants often exhibit $\mathrm{CF} > 90\%$, while intermittent solar and wind vary between $15\%$ and $45\%$).
