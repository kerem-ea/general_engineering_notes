# Higher and Lower Heating Values

Heating values describe the heat energy released during the complete combustion of a fuel.

## Definitions

- **Higher Heating Value (HHV):** Also known as gross calorific value, HHV includes the latent heat of vaporization recovered when water produced during combustion is condensed back into liquid form.
- **Lower Heating Value (LHV):** Also known as net calorific value, LHV assumes that all water produced remains as vapour in the exhaust gases, without recovering latent heat.

## Relationship

Because water condensation releases the latent heat of vaporization ($\Delta h_{\mathrm{vap}}$):

$$
\mathrm{HHV} \geq \mathrm{LHV}
$$

The difference between HHV and LHV depends directly on the hydrogen content of the fuel.
