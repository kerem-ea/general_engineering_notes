# Future Energy - Lecture 1 Formula Sheet

## Core Formulas

- **Work and Mechanical Energy:**
  $$W = \int_{s_1}^{s_2} \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{s}, \quad P = \frac{dW}{dt} = \mathbf{F} \cdot \mathbf{v}$$

- **Newton's Second Law and Momentum:**
  $$\mathbf{F} = \frac{d\mathbf{p}}{dt} = m \mathbf{a} \quad (\mathbf{p} = m \mathbf{v})$$

- **Translational Kinetic Energy:**
  $$E_k = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$$

- **Rotational Kinetic Energy:**
  $$E_{k,\text{rot}} = \frac{1}{2} I \omega^2 \quad \left(\omega = \frac{2\pi N}{60}\right)$$

- **Gravitational Potential Energy:**
  $$E_{\text{pot}} = m g h$$

- **Electrical Power and Ohm's Law:**
  $$V = I R \implies P = V I = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

- **Electromagnetic Wave Relations:**
  $$c = \lambda \nu \quad (c \approx 3.00 \times 10^8\ \mathrm{m/s})$$

- **Photon Quantization:**
  $$E_{\text{photon}} = h \nu = \frac{h c}{\lambda} \quad (h \approx 6.626 \times 10^{-34}\ \mathrm{J\cdot s})$$

- **Einstein Mass-Energy Equivalence:**
  $$E = m c^2, \quad \Delta E = \Delta m \cdot c^2$$

- **Standard Energy and Power Unit Conversions:**
  $$1\ \mathrm{kWh} = 3.60 \times 10^6\ \mathrm{J} = 3.60\ \mathrm{MJ}$$
  $$1\ \mathrm{eV} = 1.602 \times 10^{-19}\ \mathrm{J}$$
  $$1\ \mathrm{cal} = 4.184\ \mathrm{J}$$
  $$1\ \mathrm{toe} = 41.868\ \mathrm{GJ} = 11.63\ \mathrm{MWh}$$
  $$1\ \mathrm{hp} = 745.7\ \mathrm{W}$$
