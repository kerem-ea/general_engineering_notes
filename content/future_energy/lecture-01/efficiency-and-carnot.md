# Efficiency and Carnot's Limitation

Thermal efficiency measures how effectively a thermal cycle or heat engine converts supplied heat into useful work.

## General thermal efficiency

$$
\eta = \frac{\text{useful energy output}}{\text{energy input}} = \frac{W_{\mathrm{net}}}{Q_{\mathrm{in}}}
$$

## Carnot efficiency

The maximum possible theoretical efficiency for any heat engine operating cyclically between a hot reservoir at absolute temperature $T_h$ and a cold reservoir at $T_c$ is dictated by the Carnot limit:

$$
\eta_{\mathrm{Carnot}} = 1 - \frac{T_c}{T_h} = \frac{T_h - T_c}{T_h}
$$

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Temperatures $T_h$ and $T_c$ must always be specified in absolute units (Kelvin, $\mathrm{K}$).
