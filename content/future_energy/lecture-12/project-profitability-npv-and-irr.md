# Project Profitability: NPV and IRR

(Economics reading, sections 7 and 8)

Evaluating whether to construct or acquire an energy asset requires comprehensive financial metrics that balance initial capital outlays against lifetime discounted future returns.

## Net Present Value (NPV)

Net Present Value (NPV) measures the net absolute monetary wealth generated by an investment over its entire economic lifetime, discounted back to the present day:

$$
NPV = -I_0 + \sum_{t=1}^n \frac{CF_t}{(1 + r)^t}
$$

where:
- $I_0$ is the total initial capital expenditure at year $t = 0$.
- $CF_t = R_t - C_t$ is the net operational cash flow in year $t$.
- $r$ is the project discount rate.
- $n$ is the operational lifetime in years.

When operational cash flows are uniform ($CF_t = A$ for all $t$):

$$
NPV = -I_0 + A \cdot AF(r, n)
$$

### Economic Decision Rule
- **$NPV > 0$:** The project yields a higher return than the discount rate $r$. The investment creates net economic value and is accepted.
- **$NPV = 0$:** The project earns exactly the required discount rate $r$.
- **$NPV < 0$:** The investment fails to recover the opportunity cost of capital and should be rejected.

## Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

The Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is the intrinsic discount rate that drives the Net Present Value of a project to exactly zero:

$$
NPV(IRR) = 0 \iff I_0 = \sum_{t=1}^n \frac{CF_t}{(1 + IRR)^t}
$$

For a uniform annual cash flow $A$:

$$
I_0 = A \cdot AF(IRR, n) \implies AF(IRR, n) = \frac{I_0}{A}
$$

Because $AF(r, n)$ is a nonlinear transcendental function of $r$, the numerical value of $IRR$ is computed iteratively through root-finding algorithms or interpolation.

### Economic Decision Rule
- **$IRR > r$:** The project's intrinsic rate of return exceeds the cost of capital ($NPV > 0$).
- **$IRR < r$:** The project earns less than the minimum acceptable return ($NPV < 0$).

## Synthesis and Comparison of Investment Indicators

| Indicator | Mathematical Expression | Units | Strengths | Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Net Present Value (NPV)** | $-I_0 + \sum \frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t}$ | EUR | Measures absolute value creation; direct criteria for maximizing firm value. | Sensitive to chosen discount rate $r$; does not reflect capital efficiency per euro invested. |
| **Levelized Cost (LCOE)** | $\frac{\text{Annualized Costs}}{E_{\text{annual}}}$ | EUR/MWh | Transparent cost benchmarking across competing plant technologies independent of power price. | Ignores market price variability, generation timing, and value of dispatch flexibility. |
| **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)** | Rate where $NPV = 0$ | $\%$ / yr | Intuitive percentage return easily communicated to investors; independent of external $r$. | Assumes reinvestment at the IRR; can yield multiple mathematical roots for non-conventional cash flows. |
