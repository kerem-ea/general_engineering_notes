# Future Energy - Lecture 4 Formula Sheet

## Core Formulas

- **Kinetic Energy of Air Stream:**
  $$E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \quad [\text{J}]$$
- **Rotor Swept Area (Radius $R$, Diameter $D$):**
  $$A = \pi R^2 = \frac{\pi}{4}D^2 \quad [\text{m}^2]$$
- **Mass Flow Rate Through Rotor ($\rho \approx 1.23 - 1.29\text{ kg/m}^3$):**
  $$\dot{m} = \rho A v \quad [\text{kg/s}]$$
- **Theoretical Power in Undisturbed Wind:**
  $$P_w = \frac{1}{2}\dot{m}v^2 = \frac{1}{2}\rho A v^3 \quad [\text{W}]$$
- **Rotor Angular Velocity ($N$ in rpm):**
  $$\omega = \frac{2\pi N}{60} \quad [\text{rad/s}]$$
- **Blade Tip Speed ($U$):**
  $$U = \omega R = \frac{2\pi R N}{60} \quad [\text{m/s}]$$
- **Tip Speed Ratio (TSR):**
  $$\text{TSR} = \frac{U}{v} = \frac{\omega R}{v}$$
- **Power Coefficient ($C_p$):**
  $$C_p = \frac{P_{\text{extracted}}}{P_w} = \frac{P_{\text{mech}}}{\frac{1}{2}\rho A v^3}$$
- **Betz Limit (Albert Betz, 1919):**
  $$C_p(\lambda) = \frac{1}{2}(1 + \lambda)(1 - \lambda^2) \quad \left(\lambda = \frac{v_d}{v}\right)$$
  $$\text{Maximum at } \lambda = \frac{1}{3} \implies C_{p,\text{max}} = \frac{16}{27} \approx 0.593 \quad (59.3\%)$$
- **Gearbox Transmission Ratio:**
  $$\text{GR} = \frac{N_{\text{generator}}}{N_{\text{rotor}}}$$
- **Annual Energy Output (AEO):**
  $$E_{\text{annual}} = \sum_i P(v_i) \cdot h(v_i) \quad [\text{kWh}]$$
- **Capacity Factor (CF):**
  $$\text{CF} = \frac{E_{\text{annual}}}{P_{\text{rated}} \times 8760\text{ h}}$$
