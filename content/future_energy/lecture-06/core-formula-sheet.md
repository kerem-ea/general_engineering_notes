# Future Energy - Lecture 6 Formula Sheet

## Core Formulas

### 1. Mass Defect and Binding Energy
- **Mass Defect and Binding Energy:**
  $$\Delta m = \frac{E_{\text{bind}}}{c^2}$$
- **Mass Defect Using Hydrogen Atoms (electrons cancel, $N = A - Z$):**
  $$\Delta m = Z\,m_{\mathrm{H}} + N\,m_{\mathrm{n}} - m_{\text{atom}}$$
- **Binding Energy and Binding Energy per Nucleon:**
  $$E_{\text{bind}} = \Delta m \cdot 931.5\text{ MeV/u}, \qquad \frac{E_{\text{bind}}}{A} \quad [\text{MeV/nucleon}]$$
- **Conversions:**
  $$1\text{ u} = 1.6605 \times 10^{-27}\text{ kg} \equiv 931.5\text{ MeV}, \qquad 1\text{ eV} = 1.60218 \times 10^{-19}\text{ J}$$

### 2. Radioactive Decay
- **Activity Units:**
  $$1\text{ Bq} = 1\text{ s}^{-1}, \qquad 1\text{ Ci} = 3.7 \times 10^{10}\text{ Bq}$$
- **Decay Law:**
  $$N(t) = N_0\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{t/T_{1/2}}, \qquad \lambda = \frac{\ln 2}{T_{1/2}}, \qquad A = \lambda N$$
- **Beta Minus Decay:** neutron $\rightarrow$ proton + electron, so $Z \rightarrow Z+1$ and $A$ is unchanged.

### 3. Fission and Criticality
- **Induced Fission of Uranium-235:**
  $$^{235}\mathrm{U} + \mathrm{n}_{\text{thermal}} \longrightarrow {}^{236}\mathrm{U} \longrightarrow X + Y + \nu\,\mathrm{n}_{\text{fast}} + Q, \qquad Q \approx 200\text{ MeV},\ \nu = 2.43$$
- **Effective Multiplication Factor:**
  $$k_{\text{eff}} = \frac{\text{fissions in generation } i+1}{\text{fissions in generation } i}, \qquad N_n = N_0\,k_{\text{eff}}^{\,n}$$
- **Reactor State:** $k_{\text{eff}} < 1$ subcritical, $k_{\text{eff}} = 1$ critical, $k_{\text{eff}} > 1$ supercritical. Operate with $k_{\text{eff}} < 1.001$.
- **Energy per kg of Fissioned Uranium-235:**
  $$E = \frac{m}{M}\,N_A\,Q = \frac{1000\text{ g}}{235\text{ g/mol}} \cdot 6.022 \times 10^{23} \cdot 200\text{ MeV} \approx 8.2 \times 10^{13}\text{ J}$$

### 4. Cross Section and Breeding
- **Cross Section:**
  $$\sigma = \frac{\text{reaction rate per nucleus}}{\text{neutron flux}} \quad [\text{m}^2], \qquad 1\text{ barn} = 10^{-28}\text{ m}^2$$
- **Plutonium Breeding:**
  $$^{238}\mathrm{U} + \mathrm{n} \rightarrow {}^{239}\mathrm{U} \xrightarrow{\beta^-} {}^{239}\mathrm{Np} \xrightarrow{\beta^-} {}^{239}\mathrm{Pu}$$
- **Uranium Breeding from Thorium:**
  $$^{232}\mathrm{Th} + \mathrm{n} \rightarrow {}^{233}\mathrm{Th} \xrightarrow{\beta^-} {}^{233}\mathrm{Pa} \xrightarrow{\beta^-} {}^{233}\mathrm{U}$$

### 5. Enrichment
- **Natural Uranium:** $0.7\%$ uranium-235, $99.3\%$ uranium-238.
- **Diffusion Speed Ratio (Graham's law, $\mathrm{UF_6}$ masses $349$ and $352$):**
  $$\frac{v_{235}}{v_{238}} = \sqrt{\frac{352}{349}} \approx 1.0043$$
- **Enrichment Levels:** power reactors $3\text{ to }5\%$, fast reactors $10\text{ to }20\%$, LEU below $20\%$, HEU $\geq 20\%$, weapons grade $80\text{ to }90\%$.

### 6. Plant and Economics
- **Efficiency and Capacity Factor (same as [[future_energy/lecture-05/core-formula-sheet|Lecture 5 Formula Sheet]]):**
  $$\eta = \frac{E_{\text{elec}}}{E_{\text{in}}}, \qquad \text{CF} = \frac{E_{\text{elec}}}{P_{\text{rated}} \cdot 8760\text{ h}}$$
- **LCOE Split for Nuclear:** about $\frac{2}{3}$ construction, about $20\%$ operation and maintenance.
- **Fusion Reaction:**
  $$\mathrm{D} + \mathrm{T} \longrightarrow {}^{4}\mathrm{He} + \mathrm{n} + 17.6\text{ MeV}$$
