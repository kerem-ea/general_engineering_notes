## Overview

This lecture covers the thermodynamics, materials chemistry, and systems engineering of electrochemical batteries:
- Establishing fundamental cell thermodynamics, the open-circuit voltage $V_{\mathrm{oc}}$, theoretical capacity calculations ($Q_{\mathrm{spec}}$ and $Q_{\mathrm{vol}}$), C-rates, and overpotential losses.
- Surveying commercial primary cells (Leclanché, alkaline, zinc-air, lithium) and traditional secondary systems (lead-acid, nickel-cadmium, nickel-metal hydride).
- Investigating the operating mechanisms of lithium-ion batteries: the rocking-chair intercalation principle, SEI formation and dynamics, organic and polymer electrolytes, and commercial cathode/anode pairings (graphite, silicon, LTO, LCO, NMC, LFP).
- Analyzing redox flow batteries (VRFB) for decoupling grid storage power and energy, alongside next-generation solid-state, sodium-ion, lithium-sulfur, and metal-air architectures.
