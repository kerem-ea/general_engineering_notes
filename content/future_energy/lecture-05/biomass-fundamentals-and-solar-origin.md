# Biomass Fundamentals and Solar Origin

## Definitions and Global Scope

- **Biomass:** Living or recently living organic matter from plants, animals, or microorganisms.
- **Bioenergy:** Chemical energy extracted from biomass (solid, liquid, or gas).
- **Timescale Difference:** Biofuels regenerate on human timescales ($1-10\text{ yr}$), whereas fossil fuels require millions of years.

### Global Energy Share

- Primary energy supply: $\approx 57\text{ EJ/yr}$ ($\sim 10\%$ of global primary demand; see [[future_energy/lecture-01/si-units|SI Units]]).
- Renewable fraction: $\approx 70\%$ of all global renewable energy.
- Electricity generation: 3rd largest renewable electricity source after hydro and wind (see [[future_energy/lecture-04/wind-resource-and-generation-principles|Wind Resource]]).

## Solar Origin: Photosynthesis

Plants convert incident solar radiation (see [[future_energy/lecture-01/electromagnetic-radiation|Electromagnetic Radiation]] and [[future_energy/lecture-03/solar-resource-and-irradiance|Solar Irradiance]]) into chemical bonds (see [[chem/lecture-01/chemical-bonds|Chemical Bonds]]):

$$
6\mathrm{CO_2} + 6\mathrm{H_2O} + h\nu \longrightarrow \mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6} + 6\mathrm{O_2}
$$

- Reactants: $6\text{ mol }\mathrm{CO_2}$ ($264\text{ g}$) + $6\text{ mol }\mathrm{H_2O}$ ($108\text{ g}$).
- Products: $1\text{ mol glucose}$ ($\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}$, $180\text{ g}$) + $6\text{ mol }\mathrm{O_2}$ ($192\text{ g}$).
- Energy release (combustion): Reverses the reaction, returning heat, $\mathrm{CO_2}$, and $\mathrm{H_2O}$ (see [[future_energy/lecture-02/combustion-and-heating-values|Enthalpy of Combustion]]).

## Biospheric Energy Storage Flux

$$
\dot{E}_{\text{terrestrial}} \approx 2400\text{ EJ/year}, \quad \dot{E}_{\text{world demand}} \approx 600\text{ EJ/year}
$$

$$
\frac{\dot{E}_{\text{terrestrial}}}{\dot{E}_{\text{world demand}}} \approx 4
$$

Global energy crops alone have a theoretical projection of $\sim 1300\text{ EJ/yr}$ by 2050.

## Major Chemical Constituents

| Constituent       | Formula / Structure                              | Energy Role                                                     |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Glucose**       | $\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}$                          | Direct product of photosynthesis                                |
| **Cellulose**     | $(\mathrm{C_6H_{10}O_5})_n$                      | Linear glucose polymer; cell wall structural backbone           |
| **Hemicellulose** | Heteropolysaccharide                             | Branched sugar polymer; hydrolyzes easier than cellulose        |
| **Lignin**        | Complex aromatic polymer                         | Rigid binder; higher carbon content and higher energy density   |
| **Lipids (Oils)** | Triglycerides $(\text{C}_x\text{H}_y\text{O}_6)$ | High energy density seed/algae storage; feedstock for biodiesel |
