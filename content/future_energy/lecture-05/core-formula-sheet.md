# Future Energy - Lecture 5 Formula Sheet

## Core Formulas

### 1. Photosynthesis and Solar Energy Storage
- **Photosynthetic Reaction:**
  $$6\mathrm{CO_2} + 6\mathrm{H_2O} + h\nu \longrightarrow \mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6} + 6\mathrm{O_2}$$
- **Hexose Storage:** Primary energy carrier is glucose ($\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6}$, $M = 180.16\text{ g/mol}$) polymerized into cellulose and hemicellulose.

### 2. Combustion Stoichiometry and Carbon Dioxide Emission
- **Methane Combustion:**
  $$\mathrm{CH_4} + 2\mathrm{O_2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CO_2} + 2\mathrm{H_2O} + \text{Energy}$$
  Mass ratio of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ released:
  $$\frac{m_{\mathrm{CO_2}}}{m_{\mathrm{CH_4}}} = \frac{44\text{ g/mol}}{16\text{ g/mol}} = 2.75\text{ t }\mathrm{CO_2}\text{ per t }\mathrm{CH_4}$$
- **Ethanol Combustion:**
  $$\mathrm{C_2H_5OH} + 3\mathrm{O_2} \longrightarrow 2\mathrm{CO_2} + 3\mathrm{H_2O} + \text{Energy}$$
  Mass ratio of $\mathrm{CO_2}$ released:
  $$\frac{m_{\mathrm{CO_2}}}{m_{\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}}} = \frac{2 \times 44\text{ g/mol}}{46\text{ g/mol}} \approx 1.913\text{ t }\mathrm{CO_2}\text{ per t ethanol}$$
- **General Hydrocarbon Combustion Emission Factor:**
  $$\frac{m_{\mathrm{CO_2}}}{m_{\text{fuel}}} = \frac{n_{\mathrm{CO_2}} \cdot M_{\mathrm{CO_2}}}{M_{\text{fuel}}}$$

### 3. Energy Density and Heating Calculations
- **Gravimetric Energy Density ($\text{ED}_{\text{mass}}$):**
  $$\text{ED}_{\text{mass}} = \frac{\text{Energy released}}{\text{Mass of fuel}} \quad [\text{MJ/kg} = \text{GJ/t}]$$
- **Volumetric Energy Density ($\text{ED}_{\text{vol}}$):**
  $$\text{ED}_{\text{vol}} = \rho \cdot \text{ED}_{\text{mass}} \quad [\text{GJ/m}^3]$$
  where $\rho$ is the bulk density of the fuel in $\text{t/m}^3$ or $\text{kg/m}^3$.
- **Equivalent Fuel Volume for Same Energy Output:**
  $$V_2 = V_1 \cdot \frac{\text{ED}_{\text{vol}, 1}}{\text{ED}_{\text{vol}, 2}}$$
- **Thermal Heat Required ($Q$):**
  $$Q = m c \Delta T \quad [\text{J}]$$
- **Fuel Mass and Volume Required:**
  $$m_{\text{fuel}} = \frac{Q}{\text{ED}_{\text{mass}}}, \quad V_{\text{fuel}} = \frac{m_{\text{fuel}}}{\rho} = \frac{Q}{\text{ED}_{\text{vol}}}$$

### 4. Biomass Yield and Land Area
- **Area Conversion Relations:**
  $$1\text{ ha} = 10{,}000\text{ m}^2 = 10^{-2}\text{ km}^2$$
  $$1\text{ km}^2 = 100\text{ ha} = 10^6\text{ m}^2$$
- **Annual Energy Yield per Hectare ($Y_E$):**
  $$Y_E = Y_{\text{mass}} \cdot \text{ED}_{\text{mass}} \quad [\text{GJ/(ha}\cdot\text{yr)}]$$
  where $Y_{\text{mass}}$ is the dry matter crop yield in $\text{t/(ha}\cdot\text{yr)}$.
- **Total Annual Energy Harvest ($E_{\text{annual}}$):**
  $$E_{\text{annual}} = A \cdot Y_E = A \cdot Y_{\text{mass}} \cdot \text{ED}_{\text{mass}} \quad [\text{GJ/yr}]$$
- **Average Land Energy Density (Continuous Power per Area):**
  $$P_{\text{area}} = \frac{Y_E}{31.536 \times 10^6\text{ s/yr} \times 10{,}000\text{ m}^2/\text{ha}} \quad [\text{W/m}^2]$$

### 5. Biopower Plant Conversion and Efficiency
- **Plant Fuel Thermal Input Energy:**
  $$E_{\text{in}} = m_{\text{fuel}} \cdot \text{ED}_{\text{mass}} \quad [\text{GJ}\text{ or }\text{MWh}]$$
- **Thermal-to-Electrical Conversion Efficiency ($\eta$):**
  $$\eta = \frac{E_{\text{elec}}}{E_{\text{in}}} = \frac{E_{\text{elec}}}{m_{\text{fuel}} \cdot \text{ED}_{\text{mass}}}$$
- **Capacity Factor ($\text{CF}$):**
  $$\text{CF} = \frac{E_{\text{elec}}}{P_{\text{rated}} \cdot 8760\text{ h}}$$
- **Required Generator Rating ($P_{\text{rated}}$):**
  $$P_{\text{rated}} = \frac{E_{\text{elec}}}{\text{CF} \cdot 8760\text{ h}} \quad [\text{kW}\text{ or }\text{MW}]$$
- **Unit Conversions:**
  $$1\text{ kWh} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J} = 3.6\text{ MJ}$$
  $$1\text{ GWh} = 3.6 \times 10^{12}\text{ J} = 3600\text{ GJ}$$
  $$1\text{ EJ} = 10^{18}\text{ J}$$
